### 👋 About me

I'm a software engineer from **Ghana** who likes owning a product from the database to the screen. I'm the **CTO of [Purchet](https://purchet.com)**, where I design and build the whole platform, and I build **[Fontech](https://fontechsolutions.ai)**, a software, IT and career-support platform.

What I care about: systems that are **fast and dependable without being expensive to run**, money that is **always accounted for**, and code the next person can read.

- 🔭 Building **Purchet**: student accommodation in Ghana, from search to booking to payout
- 🌱 Currently learning **machine learning**
- 🎮 Also into **Flutter**, **game dev**, and **IoT**
- 🤝 Open to collaborating on interesting projects
- 📫 Reach me at **sylvesteragyiri02@gmail.com**
- 😄 Pronouns: he/him

### 🚀 What I'm building

<table>
  <tr>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">
      <h3>🏠 <a href="https://purchet.com">Purchet</a></h3>
      <p>Student accommodation marketplace for Ghana. Students find, view, book and pay for rooms; hosts list properties, manage bookings and get paid.</p>
      <ul>
        <li><b>API:</b> Laravel on PostgreSQL + Redis, role- and permission-based access for hosts, students and staff</li>
        <li><b>Money:</b> double-entry ledger with tamper-evident records, escrow, payouts, refunds and tax, on Paystack</li>
        <li><b>Real-time:</b> chat and support tickets on Cloudflare Workers with WebSockets</li>
        <li><b>Clients:</b> Next.js web app, Flutter apps for students and admins, plus an MCP server for AI tools</li>
        <li><b>Trust:</b> KYC document review, listing approval, reviews and reporting</li>
      </ul>
    </td>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">
      <h3>💼 <a href="https://fontechsolutions.ai">Fontech</a></h3>

      <ul>
        <li><b>Web:</b> Next.js 16 + React 19, Prisma on PostgreSQL, Tailwind</li>
        <li><b>Payments:</b> Stripe checkout, subscriptions, coupons and a ledger</li>
        <li><b>Auth:</b> Auth.js with passkeys (WebAuthn)</li>
        <li><b>Portals:</b> client portal, career-agent portal and a full admin CMS</li>
